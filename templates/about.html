About this application

CT‑powered CKD Stage Navigator

This demo shows how kidney segmentation and stage classification can be combined into a single, explainable workflow for rapid visual triage. It is a research prototype and not a medical device.

🧠
Backbone
U‑Net + EfficientNet‑B0
Segmentation · ROI · Stage
How the pipeline works

  • 1. Upload or sample a CT slice

    You can upload your own sagittal CT slice or click Sample test on the home page to run the model on a built‑in demo slice.

  • 2. Kidney segmentation

    A U‑Net model isolates the kidney region and produces a high‑resolution mask which is overlaid on the CT image for visual feedback.

  • 3. ROI extraction

    From the mask, a tight bounding‑box ROI is cropped and resized to a compact 224×224 view, focusing the classifier on renal parenchyma instead of background.

  • 4. Stage classification

    An EfficientNet‑based classifier predicts one of four CKD stages (1: Well preserved → 4: Advanced). Focal loss is used during training to reduce bias toward over‑represented stages.
